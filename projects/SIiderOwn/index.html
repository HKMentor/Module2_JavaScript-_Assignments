Discover sacred monuments that shaped Islamic heritage.

Kaaba, Makkah

The holiest site in Islam, visited by millions of Muslims during Hajj and Umrah. It is the qibla (direction of prayer) for Muslims worldwide.

Masjid an-Nabawi, Madinah

Established by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, it is the second holiest mosque in Islam. The Green Dome houses the Prophet’s resting place.

Dome of the Rock, Jerusalem

Built in 691 AD, it marks the place where Prophet Muhammad ﷺ ascended to the heavens during Isra and Mi'raj.

Blue Mosque, Istanbul

An Ottoman-era architectural wonder built in the 17th century. Famous for its six minarets and stunning blue interior tiles.

Great Mosque of Samarra, Iraq

Built in the 9th century, it is famous for its unique spiral minaret and was once the largest mosque in the world.

Mosque of Córdoba, Spain

A masterpiece of Islamic art and architecture. Originally a mosque, later converted into a cathedral, showcasing Islamic influence in Europe.
